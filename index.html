01 — DOSSIER
About

Most companies bring in a recruiter once the hiring problem already exists. I've spent 8+ years being the person who shows up before that, the first TA hire, the one who builds sourcing strategy, interview structure, and offer process from a blank page.

At HappyCredit I joined as employee #8 with no process to inherit; nine months later the team had scaled to 20, with every hire sourced directly. At Aster DM Healthcare, I ran technical hiring for myAster while also building an entire Marketing function that didn't exist yet, earning repeat mandates from senior stakeholders because delivery never dropped.

Before that, I spent close to four years as embedded TA for 40+ VC-backed companies at Crewscale, including Swiggy, BharatPe, and OLA Electric, closing niche technical and leadership roles across multiple markets and time zones.
